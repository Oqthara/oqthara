## What this round was worth

This is a documentation-only change and no behaviour moved, so the honest question is whether it
earns a pull request at all. It does, for one reason: Alpha 7 §5 is blocked until a record a reader
can find answers §4, and the answer currently lives in a remediation note filed under
`docs/governance/`. An ADR is the durable place.

The findings are the interesting part. Findings 1 and 2 are the same failure twice — I wrote
links and attributions from memory of what the documents *should* be called, and both were wrong in
ways that would have survived casual reading, because a plausible filename is indistinguishable
from a real one until something opens it. `check-links.py` catches the first kind. Nothing catches
the second kind; it was caught by grepping for the phrase I had attributed and finding only my own
file. A future review of any record that attributes a claim to another record should grep for the
attributed words rather than trusting the citation.

Finding 3 is a reminder that the pin mechanism is doing real work: a `DONE` row citing evidence
that does not exist at the pin is a claim a reader cannot verify, and the gate refuses it. The
tempting repair was `PENDING_EVIDENCE`, which exists precisely for this and would have been wrong —
the row needs no new evidence.
