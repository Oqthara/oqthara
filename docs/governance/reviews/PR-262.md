## What this round was worth

The previous branch re-derived five quantitative claims and found all five had decayed. It
concluded that the report decays because it works — every stale claim was invalidated by a commit
executing the report's own sequence. That conclusion had a blind spot, and this branch is what
happened when it was followed instead of restated.

Item 6 does not fit the decay model. It was not true and then false. It was never checkable, because
the section it reports on is not in the repository. Five stale counts are a maintenance problem.
A status word that says "unblocked" about an unbounded directive is worse, because it does not just
waste a re-derivation — it authorises work. Three passes noticed and deferred it, and deferral made
it look smaller rather than larger each time, which is the opposite of what happens to a recorded
finding.

The other thing worth recording is that the correction needed correcting. The first draft of this
fix got a count wrong, quoted the wrong lines for it, and pasted two command blocks that had been
reconstructed rather than captured. All three are the same failure the report charges elsewhere, and
all three were caught by running the commands rather than by re-reading the prose. Finding 2 and
finding 3 are in this record rather than tidied away for that reason.
