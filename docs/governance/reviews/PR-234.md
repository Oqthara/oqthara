## Findings

The review read `e7e297a`, which is one commit behind the change under integration. While it ran,
the implementer found the same root defect independently and was rewriting the guard around a
single chokepoint. That makes this a moving review target, and PR #233 already proposed a
documented delta re-check rule for exactly this case. The rule was applied here: every finding was
re-classified against `05a710b` rather than against the pinned commit, and each row below says
which commit actually closes it.

Three findings (1, 2 and 5) describe the statement-level design that `05a710b` deletes outright.
They are recorded as closed rather than as never having existed, because at `e7e297a` they were
true. The remaining five were real against the new tree and were fixed in `05a710b`.

## What this review did not look at

The review examined the diagnostic path and the documents describing it. It did not re-audit the
`finally` reservation that PR #233 landed, which is covered by `docs/governance/reviews/PR-233.md`.
It also did not verify the shipped-example runs, which the implementer checked separately:
`examples/hanoi.oq` prints `moves: 15` and `examples/grep.oq hanoi.oq fun` prints its two matches,
both exiting 0. Those matter here because both examples use `from` as a parameter name, and they
are the direct evidence that the ten leader words were not quietly reserved.
