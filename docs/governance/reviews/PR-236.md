## Findings

Eleven findings were consolidated from one round: zero critical, four medium, seven low. All eleven
are applied. The two `nit` findings the reviewer reported are recorded as `low`, because `nit` is not
a severity this repository's gate accepts. Rows that shared a cause are grouped, which is why the
table below carries eleven rows for eleven findings; no row combines two causes.

The two findings that mattered most are 2 and 4. Finding 2 is the sharpest: a test named
`aFieldDefaultIsNotMistakenForAType` asserted only that no exception was thrown, and the reviewer
showed that the guard it was supposedly protecting could be moved above the default expression with
the test still passing. It was named for a property it did not pin. Finding 4 showed the fix stopped
at field lists while `fun f(a: Int)` kept reporting the identical misleading bracket message that
ADR-0062 forbids, one construct to the left.

## What the round confirmed

- The guard never fires on a legal program: in a field-list position the only legal successors of a
  field name are `,` and `)`, and a colon already failed at `expect(RPAREN)`. Both calls throw before
  `fields.add`, so nothing that parsed before is refused now. This was re-verified after moving the
  guards: a 13-line program exercising defaults, map-literal defaults, comparison defaults, nested-data
  defaults, `immutable`, `implements`, `init`, methods, lambdas and construction prints byte-identical
  output on the fix build and on `origin/main`.
- `grep "new Ast.FieldDef"` finds exactly two field-list parsers, both patched; no third one was missed.
- `REFUSED` is the right matrix state, not a fudge: the brief records a DECIDED Option A and the
  refusal is pinned by a test, exactly as the legend requires.
- Claims 3 and 4 about the conformance baseline hold: `verify-conformance.py` excludes `.err` siblings
  from `executable`, so `BASELINE = {217, 208}` is untouched and still correct.

## Mutation testing

Three mutations were applied to `Parser.java` and each was confirmed present with `grep` before the
run, because a mutation placed next to a comment can leave the task `UP-TO-DATE` and report a false
negative. All three used `--rerun-tasks`.

| Mutation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Remove `rejectTypedField()` from the `data` field-list parser | 1 of 30 `ParserTest` tests failed: `aTypedFieldNamesTheDecisionInsteadOfABracket` |
| Remove all four `rejectTypedParameter()` calls | 1 of 30 failed: `aParameterNamesTheDecisionInsteadOfABracket` |
| Drop the default expression from the AST instead of storing it | 1 of 30 failed: `aFieldDefaultSurvivesAsADefault` |

The third mutation is the one finding 2 asked for: it proves the replacement test pins the property
its predecessor did not.
