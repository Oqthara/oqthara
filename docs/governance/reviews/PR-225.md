## What was examined

The reviewer read `git diff origin/main...HEAD` rather than the commit message, confirmed
`deeb889` is genuinely PR #224's merge commit and an ancestor of both `HEAD` and `origin/main`,
scanned every commit that touched the matrix to confirm `deeb889` is the first carrying all
sixteen `DONE` rows, and checked §4.2's enforcement claim against
`.github/workflows/ci.yml` and ruleset `26650412` -- which lists `ubuntu-latest / JDK 21`
among eleven other required contexts with `strict_required_status_checks_policy: true` and
`bypass_actors: []`.

It then tried to break the pin check in a throwaway clone, which is how findings 2 and 4 were
found: pinning to a commit where §4.2's evidence did not yet exist, and pinning to the branch's
own commit, both exited 0. Both were reproduced by hand before being recorded, and both are now
covered by tests that fail when the guard is removed.

Commands run: `python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests -p 'test_*.py'` (192 tests, OK) and
`python3 scripts/check-docs-sync.py` (exit 0).

## What this record is not

It is not a claim that the change is free of defects. It records one adversarial round by one
reviewer sharing the implementer's model, which is why `**Independent:**` says `no`. A reviewer
that cannot see what the implementer could not see will miss what the implementer missed; the
record exists so that what was checked, and what was not, is legible afterwards.
