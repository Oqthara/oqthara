## Findings

Three rounds were run. The first read `0635cc2` and found thirteen findings. The second read
`518cac7`, verified each of the first round's fixes, and found twelve more. The third read
`e983b77` and found nineteen, of which eleven were ways round the gate and eight were ways it
rejected a correct record. Findings are numbered continuously; a later finding that repeats an
earlier one is a fix that did not work, and is recorded as such rather than as a new item.

The third round's verdict was that the gate was not yet safe to merge as the enforcement
mechanism it claims to be. It is recorded here in those terms because the rounds happened in
that order and the earlier record was integrated in that state.

## What was examined

The second reviewer read the whole diff of `518cac7` against `0635cc2`, ran
`python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests -p 'test_*.py'`, and reproduced the deferral
bypass and the URL-as-model bypass by hand against a scratch repository before reporting them.
It verified each of the first round's thirteen findings against the code rather than against the
commit message.

Not covered: the gate's behaviour on a pull request opened by an account this repository has
never seen; the behaviour of `gh` under a token with the wrong scope other than `contents`;
and whether the machine checks measure review *quality*, which they do not and cannot. The
policy states that limitation. No human review was performed, requested, or claimed.
