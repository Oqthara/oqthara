## Notes

The gate this record covers was verified by mutation before it was committed, not only by
passing. Six mutations of the real matrix each make it exit 1: a stale probe total, a stale
executable count, a stale program total, a wrong refusal count, a conformance case that does not
exist, and a script that does not exist. Two further mutations cover the pin. The unmutated
matrix is asserted to pass by two separate tests, so a gate that rejected everything could not
present itself as thorough.

Two fenced transcripts in `alpha-7-first-report.md` and one in
`alpha-6-conformance-fallback-accounting.md` still quote 19 or 27 probes. They are records of what
a command printed at a named commit, and changing them would falsify the moment they document.
They are not drift and were deliberately not touched.
